Private day charter, arranged around you

Tower60 Charter offers one experience: a private, fully crewed day charter, staffed by your captain, crew, and private chef. There are no shared trips and no overnight stays. Every charter departs from and returns to Portofino Marina in Redondo Beach, California.

Because the yacht, crew, and chef are yours exclusively for the day, the route and length are shaped entirely around you. A typical outing covers a stretch of the South Bay coastline, with time to anchor, swim, and enjoy a chef-prepared meal before heading back in.

What's included: every charter includes your captain, a dedicated crew, and a private chef preparing a bespoke menu of premium seafood and other luxury fare, with champagne service included as standard.
Pricing: there's no set rate published here. Each charter is priced individually by the owner, reflecting your date, trip length, and any bespoke requests. Send a booking request or email alexabad@alexabadrealestate.com and you'll get a quote back.

A typical day on the water

Exact timing and stops are arranged with you directly. The outline below gives a sense of the flow.

Check-in

Meet your captain and crew at Portofino Marina. Safety briefing and boarding.

Departure

Cruise out of the marina along the coast, with stops for photos, swimming, or simply taking in the view.

Midday

Anchor in a calm spot to relax, swim, or enjoy a chef-prepared meal of premium seafood and other luxury fare, with champagne service.

Return

Cruise back to Portofino Marina and disembark.
