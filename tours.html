Private day charter, arranged around you

Tower 60 Charters runs one type of trip: a private, fully captained day charter. There is no bareboat option and no overnight charter. Every trip departs from and returns to Portofino Marina in Redondo Beach, California.

Because the boat and captain are yours for the day, the route and length are worked out directly with you. A typical outing covers a stretch of the South Bay coastline with time to anchor, swim, and relax before heading back in.

What's included: the captain is included in every charter. Food, drinks, and any extras are easy to add when you book.
Pricing: there's no set rate published here. The owner sets pricing directly based on your date, trip length, and any add-ons. Send a booking request or email alexabad@alexabadrealestate.com and you'll get a quote back.
