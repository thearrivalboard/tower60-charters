2020 Monte Carlo MC52

The Tower60 is a 2020 Monte Carlo MC52, a meticulously maintained 52-foot performance sport yacht built for effortless coastal cruising. She is based at Portofino Marina in Redondo Beach, California, and reserved for one private charter at a time.

Designed for the day

From the flybridge to the aft deck, every space aboard the Tower60 is designed for a day at sea done properly, wide open decks for lounging in the sun, and a refined interior salon for when it's time to come out of it.

A spacious sun pad, comfortable seating throughout, and an easy flow between inside and out make her equally suited to an intimate gathering or a full day of celebration.
