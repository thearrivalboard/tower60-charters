A private charter, not a production

Tower 60 Charters keeps things simple: one boat, one captain, one trip at a time. Every outing is arranged directly with us, so the day is shaped around your group rather than a fixed schedule.

Onboard, your way

A private charter should feel relaxed, not scripted. A few things that make a day aboard the Tower 60 easy.

🍴

Bring your own food and drinks

Pack a cooler, order in for pickup at the dock, or bring a full spread. The galley and aft deck are yours for the day.

🐶

Pets welcome

Well-behaved pets are welcome aboard with advance notice, so we can plan the day around them.

🚬

Smoking and vaping allowed

Permitted on the aft deck. Just let the captain know and they'll point you to the right spot.

🦶

Soft soles or bare feet

Non-marking soft-soled shoes are ideal, and bare feet are always fine once you're aboard.

Every trip: the captain runs a brief safety briefing at check-in, covering life jackets and what to expect once underway.
