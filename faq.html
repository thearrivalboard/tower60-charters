Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a day aboard the Tower 60.

FAQ

Before you book

There's no published rate. The owner determines pricing directly after hearing your date, trip length, and any add-ons. Send a booking request or email alexabad@alexabadrealestate.com and you'll get a quote back.

Sunscreen, a light jacket for the ride out, and non-marking soft-soled shoes are all good calls. Bring a swimsuit and towel if you plan to swim, and feel free to bring your own food and drinks aboard.

Guest capacity is set by the vessel's rating. We'll confirm the exact number for your date when you send an inquiry.

All Tower 60 charters are fully captained. There is no bareboat or self-skippered option, and no boating license or experience is required of guests.

If weather or sea conditions make a trip unsafe, the captain may reschedule or cancel the charter. We'll work with you directly to find a new date.

Cancellations made with reasonable notice can be rescheduled or refunded. Charters cancelled close to the date may be subject to a fee. Full terms are confirmed at the time of booking.

There is no live booking calendar. Send a booking request with your preferred date, time, and party size, and we will follow up directly to confirm availability.

Yes. Guests are welcome to bring their own food and alcohol aboard. Catering and a drinks package can also be arranged as add-ons, just mention it in your inquiry.

Well-behaved pets are welcome with advance notice. Let us know when you send your inquiry so the day can be planned around them.

Smoking and vaping are permitted on the aft deck. The captain can point you to the right spot at check-in.

Policies

Charter policies

What to know about waivers, insurance, and safety aboard the Tower 60.

Liability Waiver

Every guest reviews and signs a brief liability waiver before boarding. It takes a minute and the captain will walk you through it at check-in.

Insurance

Tower 60 Charters carries marine insurance coverage for charter operations. Policy details are available on request.

Safety Equipment & Briefing

The vessel is equipped with standard safety gear, and every trip starts with a safety briefing from the captain covering life jackets, emergency procedures, and what to expect once underway.
