Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a day aboard the Tower60.

FAQ

Before you book

There's no published rate. Each charter is priced individually, reflecting the fully crewed, bespoke nature of the experience. The owner determines pricing directly after hearing your date, trip length, and any requests. Send a booking request or email alexabad@alexabadrealestate.com and you'll get a quote back.

Sunscreen, a light jacket for the ride out, and non-marking soft-soled shoes are all good calls. Bring a swimsuit and towel if you plan to swim, and leave the food and drinks to your private chef and crew.

Guest capacity is set by the vessel's rating. We'll confirm the exact number for your date when you send an inquiry.

Every Tower60 charter comes fully crewed, including your captain and private chef. There is no self-skippered option, and no boating license or experience is required of guests.

If weather or sea conditions make a trip unsafe, the captain may reschedule or cancel the charter. We'll work with you directly to find a new date.

Cancellations made with reasonable notice can be rescheduled or refunded. Charters cancelled close to the date may be subject to a fee. Full terms are confirmed at the time of booking.

There is no live booking calendar. Send a booking request with your preferred date, time, and party size, and we will follow up directly to confirm availability.

Every charter includes a private chef who curates a bespoke menu for your group, from premium seafood to other luxury fare, with champagne service included as standard. If you'd like to bring a specific bottle or have a particular request, just mention it in your inquiry and we'll work it in.

Pets are not permitted aboard the Tower60.

No smoking or vaping is permitted aboard the Tower60.

Policies

Charter policies

What to know about waivers, insurance, and safety aboard the Tower60.

Liability Waiver

Every guest reviews and signs a brief liability waiver before boarding. It takes a minute and the captain will walk you through it at check-in.

Insurance

Tower60 Charter carries marine insurance coverage for charter operations. Policy details are available on request.

Safety Equipment & Briefing

The vessel is equipped with standard safety gear, and every trip starts with a safety briefing from the captain covering life jackets, emergency procedures, and what to expect once underway.
